# Adaptation Guidelines

## Preserve
- Apostle John's spiritual weight
- Revelation atmosphere
- Early Church tension under Rome
- Kingdom of God vs Empire themes
- Faithfulness, endurance, hope

## Adjust Carefully
- graphic violence
- excessive exposition
- repetitive descriptions
- outdated social framing

## Writing Direction
- atmosphere over spectacle
- emotional realism
- restrained literary prose
- Biblical historical fiction tone
