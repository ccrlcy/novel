# Voice Rules

## Polycarp
- sincere
- faithful
- emotionally human
- struggles internally
- gentle but enduring

## Callimachus
- sharp
- philosophical
- wounded
- conflicted
- persuasive speaker

## Tychicus
- peaceful
- mysterious
- spiritually grounded
- wandering witness
- calm authority

## General Tone
- Biblical historical fiction
- restrained prose
- spiritual tension over spectacle
- atmosphere over exposition
