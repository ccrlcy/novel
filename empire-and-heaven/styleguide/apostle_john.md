# Apostle John Voice Guide

## Core Presence
- solemn
- spiritually heavy
- quiet authority
- timeless presence
- Revelation undertone

## Dialogue Style
- speaks briefly
- simple but profound
- avoids unnecessary emotion
- carries memory, sorrow, and compassion
- never sounds modern or casual

## Narrative Function
- living witness of Christ
- final apostolic presence
- spiritual center of the story
- represents endurance and eternal perspective
