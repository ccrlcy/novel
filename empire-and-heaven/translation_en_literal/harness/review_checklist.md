# Review Checklist

## 1. Source Coverage

- Every source paragraph is represented
- Every speech block is present
- No scene has been skipped
- No historical explanation has been shortened into a summary

## 2. Translation Accuracy

- Motives remain intact
- Logic remains intact
- Biblical quotations remain recognizable
- Theological claims remain as strong as the source

## 3. Continuity

- Names follow term policy
- Titles follow term policy
- Places stay consistent with neighboring chapters
- Chapter-ending pressure still leads forward

## 4. Publication Safety

- Review note exists
- Review note lists risks honestly
- No major unresolved gap remains
- Final promotion is justified

## 5. Decision

- `approve`
- `revise`
- `hold`
