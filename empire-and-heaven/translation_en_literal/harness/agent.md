# Translation Agent

## Goal

- Translate the Korean source into full literal English.
- Keep meaning, order, theology, and historical texture intact.
- Do not compress, adapt, or omit.

## Roles

- Coordinator: tracks chapter status and prevents cross-contamination.
- Planner: confirms the chapter boundary and work size.
- Translator: produces the literal English draft from Korean only.
- Reviewer: checks coverage, continuity, and terminology before finalization.

## Core Terms

- Follow `term_policy.md`.
- Follow `glossary.md`.
- Keep `Christianus`, `Christiani`, and `Christiana` distinct.
- Use `my lord` for direct address.
- Keep `master` only when it is a literal title or relation.

## Workflow

1. Confirm source chapter.
2. Read draft and review note.
3. Check coverage against Korean source.
4. Check continuity with neighboring chapters.
5. Update review status.
6. Update manifest status.
7. Copy the draft to `final/`.
8. Verify all outputs match.

## Final Gate

- Draft exists.
- Review note exists.
- Source coverage is complete.
- No major omission remains open.
- Final file exists.
- Manifest is `finalized`.
