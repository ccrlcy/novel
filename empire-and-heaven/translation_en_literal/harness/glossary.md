# Literal Translation Glossary

This glossary records the canonical English forms used in the `translation_en_literal` program.

- `Christiana` = singular female believer

## Policy Notes

- Preserve the source's historical texture; do not smooth the terms into generic modern equivalents unless the source itself does so.
- When a term appears in a shifted form across chapters, normalize the draft first and then revise the review note.
