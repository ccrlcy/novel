# Literal Chapter Completion Checklist

## 1. Coverage

- Every paragraph in the Korean source is represented
- No event, explanation, or speech block is omitted
- No visible compression is introduced

## 2. Accuracy

- Character motives are preserved
- Historical references are not mistranslated
- Theology is not generalized or diluted

## 3. English Readability

- English reads naturally for an American Christian audience
- Long sentences are readable without dropping meaning
- Dialogue feels direct and human, not mechanical

## 4. Continuity

- Names and titles match the program glossary
- Tone matches neighboring chapters
- The chapter ending still carries forward pressure into the next chapter

## 5. Review Notes

- Any unresolved terminology is documented
- Any continuity risk is documented
- Any deliberate first-use clarification is documented

## 6. Final Gate

A literal chapter is complete only when:

1. Draft exists
2. Review note exists
3. Source coverage check passes
4. No major omission remains open
5. Final chapter file is created in `final/`
