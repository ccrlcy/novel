# Volume I Chapter 2 Segment Plan

Source: `chapters/상_02_낯선_사람들.md`

Target segments: 5

## Segment 1

- Arrival at Ephesus by sea
- First sight of the city, harbor monument, and festival crowd
- End anchor: Artemis procession fully comes into view

## Segment 2

- Artemis statue, priests, priestesses, and festival route
- Harbor sacrifice sequence and public frenzy
- End anchor: Andronicus is formally received at the harbor

## Segment 3

- Procession through Harbor Avenue
- Ephesus luxury, gambling, baths, and civic culture
- End anchor: the city as spectacle and system is firmly established

## Segment 4

- Andronicus questions Lucius about Ephesus
- Education, social ambition, and Hesthenes's observations
- Tychicus reappears in the background
- End anchor: approach toward the imperial temple and city center

## Segment 5

- Domitian temple scene
- Governor's residence and official reception
- Political conversation and rising tension beneath civic courtesy
- End anchor: chapter close inside the governor's sphere

## Merge Notes

- Preserve the shift from dazzled arrival to spiritually corrupted spectacle
- Do not compress ritual or city-description blocks
- Keep the return of Tychicus understated but noticeable
