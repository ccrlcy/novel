# Volume I Chapter 3 Segment Plan

Source: `chapters/상_03_어둠을_헤치고.md`

Target segments: 5

## Segment 1

- Great theater at dusk
- Polycarp, Callimachus, and Prochorus waiting
- Tychicus is identified through the fish-sign exchange
- End anchor: identity and first recognition are secured

## Segment 2

- Tychicus recalls Onesimus, Timothy, and old Ephesus believers
- Apostolic memory deepens through names and tears
- End anchor: Tychicus begins recounting Paul's ministry in Ephesus

## Segment 3

- Paul's return to Ephesus
- Apollos, Tyrannus, miracles, and the growth of the church
- Demetrius riot and the great theater upheaval
- End anchor: Paul leaves Ephesus after the crisis

## Segment 4

- Luke's record, collected church documents, and apostolic legacy
- Tychicus sees the younger generation preserving the faith
- Artemis procession passes again in contrast to the hidden church
- End anchor: movement toward the house-church destination

## Segment 5

- Discussion of apostasy, Mary, persecution, and the broken history of Ephesus believers
- The chapter resolves into hidden-faith continuity rather than public triumph
- End anchor: chapter close at the threshold of the deeper church story

## Merge Notes

- Preserve the contrast between loud pagan spectacle and quiet apostolic continuity
- Keep Tychicus's memory voice calm, grave, and personal
- Do not flatten the documentary references into summary
