# Chapter Completion Harness

This checklist defines when an English adaptation chapter can be marked complete.

## 1. Source Fidelity
- Core events from the Korean source are preserved.
- Character motivations are not reversed or diluted.
- Compression is allowed only when it improves modern English readability without damaging plot or theology.
- Added material must support the existing thematic direction and not contradict later chapters.

## 2. English Literary Quality
- No Korean sentence carryover.
- No stiff translationese.
- Narrative prose reads as biblical historical fiction, not as a literal translation.
- Sentences vary in length but avoid over-explaining.
- Scene transitions are clear and emotionally motivated.

## 3. Biblical and Theological Tone
- Kingdom of God vs Empire remains central.
- Christian hope is framed as endurance and faithfulness, not sensationalism.
- Scripture-adjacent language should echo NIV readability with NASB-style gravity.
- No doctrinal claims should be carelessly modernized or exaggerated.

## 4. Historical Texture
- Roman titles, social customs, city geography, and public religion are used consistently.
- Historical explanation should serve the scene rather than interrupt it.
- Violence, sexuality, and ritual spectacle are adapted with restraint for modern readers.

## 5. Character Voice
- Andronicus: ambitious, wounded, observant, beginning to sense Rome's decay.
- Hesthenes: sharp, loyal, wry, socially perceptive.
- Tychicus: quiet spiritual authority, restrained sorrow, no melodrama.
- Drusiana references: remembered with tenderness and distance.

## 6. Motif Continuity
- Wind, sea, silence, empire, truth, and hidden kingdom imagery must remain coherent.
- Chapter ending should carry forward tension into the next chapter.

## 7. Final Gate
A chapter is complete only when it passes:
- Adaptation pass
- Theology pass
- Historical pass
- Character voice pass
- Prose polish pass
- Continuity pass
- Final editor pass
